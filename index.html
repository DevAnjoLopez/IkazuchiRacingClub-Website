The club

Driven by the storm.

Ikazuchi Racing Club was established in 2024 by a group of passionate drivers who share a love for racing and the thrill of the track. Our team is dedicated to pushing the limits of performance and skill, competing in various racing events and tournaments in the Midnight Racing: Tokyo Community. We believe that racing brings people together, Midnight Racing or not, and we strive to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members, regardless of experience level. Witness the power of the lightning!
