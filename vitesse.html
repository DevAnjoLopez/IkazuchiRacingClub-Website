Vitesse

“I don’t keep up with my rivals, ’cause my rivals keep up with me.”

Vitesse, better known to everyone as Sally, is famous for his iconic taglines, questionable antics, impeccable fashion sense, and extensive collection of burner phones. A man from the lower side of Beverly Hills, he lives like he belongs in the Stone Age while somehow owning every designer bag he can get his hands on in Japan.

After a brief hiatus from the team, he’s recently been spotted climbing the billboards once again, clocking impressive times and making his long-awaited comeback as a newly renowned racer. Known for taking daring cars wherever he goes, you might catch him cruising through Tokyo in his Brooklands one moment, then tearing up the streets of Ichikawa in his Diablo VT the next.

He may be rocking pink sweatsuits, but never let the outfit fool you.
