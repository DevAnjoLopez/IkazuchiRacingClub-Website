Driver story

BigHotBmxBoy

BigHotBmxBoy is a former racer who turned his connection to car culture into a creative media outlet. Time behind the wheel taught him to notice the details of driving, from weight transfer and glowing brakes to the intensity of a close battle. That experience gives his motorsport videos an authentic feel.

Working under his creative brand, Pancake Visuals (formerly Tokyo Auto Scene), BigHotBmxBoy produces automotive films, event recaps, and feature montages. His work highlights both the look of tuned vehicles and the passion, effort, and stories of the drivers behind them, making Pancake Visuals a media hub for enthusiasts.
