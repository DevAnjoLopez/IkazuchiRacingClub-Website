Driver story

Mrmyth456

MrMyth456 stands as a legendary figure in the Filipino racing scene, with a legacy built on raw speed, extraordinary discipline, and surgical line precision. During his peak active years, he was a quiet force on the track who let his lap times do the talking. His understanding of car dynamics helped him carry astonishing momentum through high-speed sections without unsettling the chassis.

MrMyth456's name became synonymous with dominance on the technical C1 Highway Route. His crowning achievement, a time of 2:58.865 on the Clockwise Loop, remains one of the most respected time-attack benchmarks in the community, a testament to his execution through narrow walls and high-speed sweepers.

Though retired from competitive street battles in Midnight Racing: Tokyo, his love for motorsports remains strong. He continues to apply his speed and precision across other racing titles, frequently turning laps in Assetto Corsa.
